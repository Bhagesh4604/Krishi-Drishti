🔴 Research Motivation
The $2 Billion Carbon Paradox
Why 86% of India's 146 million farmers are locked out of the global voluntary carbon market
💸 Astronomical Audit Cost: $1,500 – $3,000 per Farm

Conventional MRV demands depth-stratified soil core extraction, chemical lab spectrography (Walkley-Black oxidation), certified VVB auditor travel. For a 1-hectare farm generating ~3–5 tCO₂e at ₹1,200/tonne, verification alone costs 10–30× the gross revenue.

Lab Testing: ₹1.5L–₹2.5L Auditor Travel Cost Annual Lab Renewal
⏰ Crippling 6–18 Month Verification Cycle

Physical soil coring, courier delays to certified labs, 3-layer bureaucratic audit review, and VVB sign-off add up to 6 to 18 months before any payout. Subsistence farmers cannot wait a year and a half for supplementary income.

Field Coring: 2 weeks Lab Analysis: 1–3 months VVB Audit: 6–12 months
🌾 Mass Exclusion: 86.2% of Farmers (<2 ha)

India has 146M agricultural landholdings of which 126M are under 2 hectares. The $2B global voluntary carbon market (Hamrick, 2023) has developed entirely around large corporate estates and industrial plantations.

🛰️ Remote Sensing Core
Sentinel-2 Multispectral Data Pipeline
Replacing $3,000 laboratory soil spectrography with free ESA satellite Earth Observation via Google Earth Engine
01
Level-2A BOA Imagery Retrieval (GEE API)

Sentinel-2 Level-2A Bottom-of-Atmosphere reflectance with atmospheric corrections applied. Queried via ee Python API with GEE Service Account Auth at 10m × 10m spatial resolution, 5-day global revisit frequency.

02
QA60 Cloud Bitmasking + Atmospheric Filtering

The satellite's native QA60 bitmask band classifies every pixel as cloud/cirrus/clear. Opaque clouds (bit 10) and cirrus (bit 11) are automatically masked — this eliminates all atmospheric scattering distortion.

03
90-Day Rolling Median Composite (Vaudour 2022)

Aggregates all clear-sky observations within a 90-day sliding window into a single clean composite image. Guarantees at least 1–3 unobstructed observations per plot without introducing seasonal vegetation bias.

04
Bare-Soil NDVI < 0.3 Threshold + 12-Band Vector

Dense crop canopy masks the soil spectral signal. By filtering pixels where NDVI < 0.3 during fallow/post-harvest windows, we isolate pure bare topsoil reflectance and extract the 12-dimensional feature vector.

🛡️ Viva Defense — Top 4 Examiner Questions
Q1: How can satellite replace physical soil lab testing? A: BSI, SWIR Band B11, and Red-Edge B5 capture organic matter molecular vibrational absorption. Our Stacking Ensemble with 50 ground-truth calibration points achieves RMSE=1.52 g/kg, within Verra's ≤3.0 g/kg dMRV tolerance threshold.
Q2: How do you prevent greenwashing and false claims? A: Additionality Engine checks ICAR/NABARD district-level adoption data. If >50% already use the practice, the project is auto-rejected. 3 years of pre-enrollment historical satellite NDVI baseline also locks the baseline.
Q3: Why hash chaining instead of full on-chain Ethereum? A: Ethereum gas fees of $0.50–$5.00 per transaction would consume 5–15% of smallholder earnings. SHA-256 local chaining provides identical tamper-evidence at zero cost. Daily Merkle roots can optionally anchor to Polygon Layer-2.
Q4: Why 88.1% when CNN papers show 98%? A: Those achieve 98% on single-crop controlled-light datasets. Our model covers multi-crop diverse field lighting AND is the FIRST in literature connected to a carbon credit engine with organic remediation compliance checks.
