Krishi-Drishti: How to Use the App (Farmer's Perspective)

Here is exactly what you need to do in the Krishi-Drishti app, step-by-step, just as a farmer:

1. Log In to the App
* Open the Krishi-Drishti app.
* Type in your Mobile Number.
* Enter the OTP sent via SMS to securely log into your account.
* (Optional) Tap the language icon at the top to switch to Hindi or your local language.

2. Draw Your Farm Boundary (Very Important!)
* Tap the Field (or Map) icon at the bottom of the screen.
* Tap "Mark Field".
* A satellite view of your land will appear. Tap the edges of your actual physical farm to draw a boundary around it (like drawing a fence on the screen).
* Tap "Save Plot". (This tells the app exactly where to monitor via satellite for your carbon credits!)

3. Check Daily Weather & News
* On the main Home Dashboard, check your daily localized weather (temperature, soil temp, rainfall).
* Read local agricultural news below the weather to stay updated.

4. Scan Your Crops (Evidence Building)
* Walk into your field.
* Tap the floating Green Plus (+) Button or the Camera/Scanner icon.
* Take a clear picture of your actual crop leaves.
* The AI will instantly tell you if the crop is healthy or diseased.
* (Secret Benefit: Doing this occasionally proves to the system that you are actively and safely farming!)

5. Claim Your Carbon Credits!
* After the season ends, tap on the Profile or Carbon icon to open the Carbon Vault.
* Read your current stats and tap the "Start Earth Engine Audit" button.
* Wait a moment while the satellites automatically check your farm's health over the last few months.
* Cha-ching! The app will mint "ACT" (Agri Carbon Tokens) and add them to your Carbon Earnings balance on the home screen.

6. Sell Your Produce & Credits (Mandi)
* Tap the Mandi (Store) icon at the bottom.
* Add a listing to sell your harvested crops.
* Corporate buyers can also see your verified Carbon Credits here and purchase them directly from you, sending the money straight to your registered bank account!

***
In short: Just map your farm once, use the camera to check your plants occasionally, and collect your carbon money at the end of the season!
